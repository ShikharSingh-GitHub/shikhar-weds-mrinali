Our Love Story

A doctor, a developer, and a love story with zero patience for delays.

  1. Chapter One

    Two Different Worlds

    She spent her days healing people in busy hospital corridors. He spent his nights fixing code that refused to behave. Neither was looking for love, but love was looking for them.

  2. Chapter Two

    The First Hello

    One conversation was all it took. A polite hello turned into talks that ran past midnight, and suddenly every day had a favourite part.

  3. Chapter Three

    Between Shifts & Sprints

    Night shifts and release deadlines couldn’t keep them apart. Calls squeezed in between rounds, late dinners after deploys, and a lot of “just five more minutes”.

  4. Chapter Four

    The Families Said Yes

    With the blessings of the Singh and Chaddha families, a tilak, and a lot of happy tears, two hearts became one promise.

  5. 12 December 2026

    Forever Begins

    With Agni as our witness and all of you beside us, we take our seven pheras in Indore. This is the chapter we’ve been waiting for.

Getting to Indore

Travel & Stay

India’s cleanest city and the food capital of Madhya Pradesh is waiting for you.

By Air

Devi Ahilya Bai Holkar Airport (IDR) has direct flights from Delhi, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad and other major cities. Taxis and app cabs are available at the airport.

By Train

Indore Junction (INDB) is the main railway station and is well connected across India. Cabs and autos are easy to find outside.

By Road

Indore is well connected by highway to Bhopal, Ujjain and Ahmedabad. Hotel Sayaji is in Vijay Nagar, the city’s busy commercial hub.

Accommodation

Coming from out of town? Mention your arrival date and stay needs in the RSVP message and the family will get in touch to help with rooms and pickups.

Weather

December in Indore is lovely: pleasant sunny days and cool evenings that can dip to around 10°C. Pack a shawl or jacket for the night functions.

Explore & Eat

Try poha-jalebi for breakfast, the late-night food stalls of Sarafa Bazaar and Chhappan Dukan, and visit Rajwada or Lal Bagh Palace. Ujjain’s Mahakaleshwar is a short drive away.

FAQs

When and where are the celebrations?

From Wednesday 9 to Saturday 12 December 2026 in Indore. The Ganesh Pujan, Mehendi and Haldi (9–10 Dec) are at Shadiwala Ghar, Khandwa Road. The Ring Ceremony, Sangeet, Wedding and Reception (11–12 Dec) are at Hotel Sayaji, Vijay Nagar. See the complete itinerary.

What time should I arrive for the wedding?

The Baraat starts at 10:00 AM and the Jaimala & Mangal Pheras begin at 12:15 PM on Saturday, 12 December. Do join the baraat if you can. It’s the most fun part of the morning!

Is there a dress code?

Yes. Ethnic or Indo-Western for the Engagement, Western or Indo-Western for the Sangeet & DJ Night, Pure Traditional for the Pheras and Pure Western for the Reception. Suggested colours are in the Dress Code section.

Will meals be served?

Absolutely. This is Indore! Lunch is served from 1:00 PM on 10, 11 and 12 December, and dinner from 8:00 PM on 10, 11 and 12 December. Please share your meal preference in the RSVP.

Can I bring my family or a plus-one?

Please include everyone attending in the “Number of Guests” field of your RSVP so we can plan seating and meals for all.

I’m travelling from another city. Can you help?

Of course. Mention your arrival details in the RSVP message and the family will reach out. Indore is easy to reach by air (IDR) and rail (Indore Junction). See Travel & Stay.

Can I take photos and post them?

Please do, and tag them with #ShikharWedsMrinali! During the pheras, we’d love for you to put phones away and be fully present with us. Our photographers have it covered.

What about gifts?

Your presence and blessings are the greatest gift we could ask for.
